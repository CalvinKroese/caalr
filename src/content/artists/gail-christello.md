I have had a creative spirit my whole life. Growing up in a family of artists has given me a curious nature of what I could create.

My passion turned out to be watercolor, with fluidity and the ability to paint a transparent quality that acrylic and oils can’t give. Beaches, landscapes, and travels to Caribbean islands have been my favorite palettes.

Collages and mixed media using acrylics is also an exciting medium and gives a freedom to create textures and depth and gives another element to my artwork.

My art has been featured in the Venice Art Center and sells in the gallery. Also, featured in the Englewood Art Center as well as sold in numerous local art shows and can be found in private collections.

It is such a privilege to be able to share my art with others and to meet other artists and art appreciators.

Gail Cristello
