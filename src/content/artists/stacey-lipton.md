After recently retiring from my interior design business, I tried to redirect my creativity and design passion into creating art that is both beautiful and accessible. I am drawn to watercolor for its remarkable ability to build translucent layers of color allowing light, texture, and splatter to bring each piece to life.

 

My work often features vibrant sunset hues, the lush flora and fauna that defines Florida, and inspiring travel scenes from around the world.

Through my paintings, I aim to share moments of color, calm, and joy-

art that feels uplifting and approachable for all who experience it.
