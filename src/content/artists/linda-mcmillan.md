Linda McMillan is a Florida-based fiber artist specializing in handcrafted handbags created through innovative fabric and sewing techniques. With a BA in Art Education and a background in higher education, she combines craftsmanship with creative design to produce one-of-a-kind statement pieces, often incorporating canvas art.

Since retiring, Linda has focused on expanding her work in fiber art. Her creations have included handmade American Girl doll furniture, with several pieces featured in The Unbreakable Kimmy Schmidt. A member of the Creative Arts Association, she is also known as “The Gnome Lady” for her popular handmade figures, though her current passion is designing unique purses.
