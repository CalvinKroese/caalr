	Teaching Art was my passion for 35 years.  I taught in Iowa, Nebraska, Illinois and Florida.

In Florida I taught Middle School for 15 years.  

	When I retired from teaching, I wanted to learn about making jewelry.  We spent some of the year in North Carolina.  It is a state that celebrates art.  Where we stayed, there were two schools for

learning about jewelry.  I attended both for many years of classes.  John Campbell Folk School in Brasstown, NC and William Holland School of Lapidary Arts in Young Harris, Georgia.  

 	 I have been making jewelry for 20 years.  In Murphy, North Carolina I belonged to a group of artists that maintained a store and sold the art work of their members. They have grown and still remain.  In Bradenton, Florida I joined a group at Lakewood Ranch, Creative Art Association of Lakewood Ranch.  I have been a member here for at least 15 years.  

 	I enjoy working and making friends with my fellow artists.  It is always a pleasure to see customers appreciate your work.  Thank you to all.
