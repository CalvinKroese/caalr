Benise Jones a member of the Lakewood Ranch Creative Arts Association since November 2024 originally from Maryland is a creative Journal Book Designer and More.  

Benise transforms journals into wonderful art designs, painting and designing each journal along with making bookmarks and post it notebooks.  She also incorporates beautiful photos taken around the world on the journal covers.

Her love for different art, writing and photography encouraged her to make these wonderful journals and more.  She enjoys creating art in various forms and fashions and always looks forward to introducing new designs.
